# THE GREY WOLF — a Soul Land 2 fan fiction

> **Era:** Soul Land II: The Unrivaled Tang Sect · **Beside canon:** the
> same years as Huo Yuhao · **Status:** six chapters written — Arc 1
> (Grey Ridge) complete, Arc 2 (the road to Shrek) road craft — Chapter 6 The Hem Road (all basics 100% MASTERED High in daily life, Spirit Sea 850, body ~500kg, Storm Frost Ghost Wolf ice+wind High, Ghost Veil 3 clones golden lock toughest skull tofu waist, Storm Step wind blade burst wings flight, Ring Veil hides purple as yellow); releases through v0.6.9-f22; Chapter 7 — Shrek City arrival — next, at the author's word.

A man who counted things for a living dies on Earth, and is born again on
the Douluo Continent with a thousand stories in his head — including the
one he is now standing inside. At six, in a hunter village on the edge of
the Great Star Dou Forest, Ye Cang awakens the Grey Wolf and one rank of
innate soul power: the village's verdict is a shrug. That night, something
quiet wakes behind his eyes and begins to count him — slots, meters, and a
full status panel the stories of his first life would call a System. He
calls it the Ledger.

He knows this world's book. He knows what the continent is about to do,
what rises in the east, what a ten-year-old boy with Spirit Eyes will find
alone in the forest in four years, and exactly what a rank-one talent is
worth in a world sorted by speed. He has read the ending. Now he intends
to live past his page in it — beside the canon story, on his own road, at
Shrek Academy, in the age of soul tools.

## The working laws (carried whole from the first serial, plus its lessons)

Plain speech; panels cold and quiet at key beats — **and the FULL status
panel at every gate**; every number a current reading, nothing fixed;
every art its own clock; everything that grows feeds every open meter;
100% is a gate, never a resting state; the world defines roads, the System
never invents them; receipts demanded for every canon claim; canon goes as
canon goes where the story does not touch it, and what the story touches,
changes — computed and logged.
