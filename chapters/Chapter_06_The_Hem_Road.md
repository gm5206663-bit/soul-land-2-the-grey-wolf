# Chapter 6 — The Hem Road

The hem road was not a road. It was what was left when forest decided not to be forest for a while. A strip of hard ground between trees and tilled ground. Feet and cart rims and weather had beaten it flat. Grass grew up its middle and was beaten down again. Villages knew it. Hunters knew it. No one owned it. That was why his father had said hem, and north, and never in trees.

He walked it north.

First morning was cold and thin. Ledger was there, like floor. He looked and found it honest. Level thirty. Two rings aging in seats, purple thousand-year, concealed as yellow via Veil of Ghost High. Hair showed grey with frost tint. Amber eyes turned ice-amber. Hands became claws. Nails sharpened like daggers. Faint grey fur showed on arms. Cold air left his mouth. Wind curled around his feet. Beast possession. Attributes superimposed upon body. Changes grew more pronounced as cultivation rose. Body was robust released or not. At eleven, possession held whole morning and asked for more.

Frame had arrived as freight across a season. That freight now walked. Height taller than village boys same age. Shoulders broader. Wrist no longer knew sprain.
