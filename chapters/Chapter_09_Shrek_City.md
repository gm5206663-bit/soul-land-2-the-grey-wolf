# Chapter 09 — Shrek City

Shrek City woke early, not like Grey Ridge, where dogs woke first, then wind, then silence with teeth. City woke with carts, hammers, voices, soul power in air thicker than ridge, cold lamps burning without fire that never slept.

He woke in loft with city noise below and wind through eaves above, ledger was there, like floor, level thirty. Two rings aging in seats, bloodlines, two slots, no update, no just gain, so no full panel. That was the rule the author gave, normally you can check in status file everything when needed. He checked in mind and found honest.

He left inn before light. Old woman who kept it had not asked where boy eleven learned to set chair true. He did not tell her, he paid with mended chair and straightened table, not coin, because hands knew work.

Inner city north was different alphabet than hem road, stone road became wider, ruts deeper, traffic more. Soul tool shops opened early, cold lamps with engraved metal, lit from inside, steady. He had seen one in Anlin window, cost more than forty roofs. Small quiet first line of war east was building, east already building it street by street army by army. He let it go and walked, he knew what it was, he had read future twice on Earth. Future was engraved metal burning without fire, he said nothing, knowledge lives in choices, never in mouth.

He walked with quiet step, footwork was one line now, not three. Walking and running and mountain stride had all been footwork, each had hit a hundred at eight, nine, ten. They did not go to High alone, they fused into one quiet step. Which fool masters so many step techniques separately, they fuse, so he walked inner city crowd with quiet step, seen-late. Street taught it differently than ridge, on ridge, you learned where rock would give. In town, you learned where crowd would give.

Shrek Academy gates came after third bell, he heard them before seeing. Gate hinges, students breathing, soul power crystal humming, river of people moving, that was senses at work.

Gates were not city East Hem Gate, east Hem Gate was built for carts, academy gates were built for story. Ten meters high, dark wood with iron, with words carved that ten thousand years had not worn smooth: Shrek Academy. Beyond them, courtyard, buildings, lake in distance, willows, students in green and yellow, he had read about it twice. Book this world lived inside had described it as continent's first academy, city that grew around it for ten thousand years, Tang Sect in decline beside it across fence. Now he stood before it, eleven years old, road coat weighted at hem, father's spear on shoulder, two yellow hundred-year rings aging in seats, bloodlines, fused Hunt in third seat, whole counted life folded shut like Old Kuo's book.

Line for exam was already long. Boys and girls his age, some older, some with parents, some alone, all with martial souls awake and innate measured. He joined line, patience made waiting honest. He could sit or stand whole short hour, spear across knees, counting cart ruts and wind and field birds without fidget, without being told to. Body outlasted boy in it, something old and wolfish held sitting boy could not. That was ghost line at work, patient, willing to endure attrition until prey tires.

He kept rings hidden, to world, two yellow hundred-year rings showed, yellow within limits, wolf ice thin. A boy showing two yellow rings at eleven would be noticed to stand in line. He would be taken to inner courtyard and asked questions no eleven-year-old could answer honestly. He learned that at second village when boy stared too long at his hands, so he showed two yellow. Exam moved in three parts, as it had in book he read. First part soul power crystal, second part body and martial soul, third part combat and character. He had read it, but he lived it as ridge boy would.

First part: crystal, examiner was older woman in green, with tool martial soul, level maybe Soul King, bored. She waved him to crystal, he put hand on it, as he had at six in stone room. At six, crystal had said level one, innate one, wolf, ice thin, village shrug. Now pool was dark dense, water of deeper well fed by bloodlines and seventeen poured levels, reborn mind wasted no hour, method ran while he carried water while he sat black pools while he slept, where other souls worked one hour his worked every one and it compounded. "Eleven."

"Wolf, beast-type, power Attack, ice plus wind.".

He called them, two yellow hundred-year within limits, wolf ice thin, light came. Second part: body and martial soul. Yard with mats, with older students watching, with teacher with beast martial soul bear maybe, he called grey on him. Silent hunt. Teacher looked at claws, at fur, at wind around feet, at ice plus wind. He nodded once, as father had nodded once when boy read wind right, and that nod held more weight than other men's speeches.

"Strength?" teacher asked.

He lifted stone at yard edge, not with show, but with point leading, as spear flow taught. Thrust recover low guard, boar point leads moving thing hand's width, complete style, stone settled. Teacher did not ask how boy eleven did it, village knows when not to ask.

"Pass," teacher said, "Third part tomorrow, combat and character, be here at bell.".

He left yard. Students watched him because frame freight height, amber eyes, grey tint, dogs no lift, wood passing at peace. He did not watch them, he walked with quiet step, ground did not know he passed.
