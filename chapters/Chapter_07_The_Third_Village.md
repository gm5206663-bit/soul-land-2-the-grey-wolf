# Chapter 07 — The Third Village

The hem road held its shape because no one had told it to be a road. It was the place where forest stopped taking rent and fields had not yet started. Hard ground, kept honest by feet and cart rims and weather, grass thin in the middle where wheels beat it twice. Ye Cang walked it because his father said hem north never in trees, and because a road no one owned asked no questions.

He left the second village before light. The loft had been warm with suppers below and cold with wind through eaves above. He paid with a mended fence and a straightened gate, not coin. The woman who kept the loft did not ask where a boy of eleven learned to hang a gate so it swung true. He did not tell her.

The third village came by noon. It was larger than the first two, almost a market town, with a low wall built for goats, not war. The hem road became a lane between houses, then a street with stalls. He heard it before he saw it. That was Keen Senses Mid. Five Senses Low had been a child's nose and ears. At a hundred it became Keen Senses Mid, different name, different work. At a hundred again it would become Spirit Eye High, different name again. No terminal, every evolution different name. Now Keen Senses let him hear a loose rim three carts ahead, a dog favoring left hind leg, river before bend because air changed.

He did not use Ghost Veil. Road wanted Patience as Limb Mid, not burst. Stillness Low had been sitting in thorn thickets without fidgeting. At a hundred it became Patience as Limb Mid, different name. At a hundred again it would become Void Stillness High, different name. Sitting on hem road edge with spear across knees, counting cart ruts and wind and field birds without moving, kept Patience as Limb honest. Ghost Veil was Light of Netherworld Low → Netherlight Mid → Ghost Veil High → Ghost Storm Veil Top, different name each grade. It was for being seen late. Here being unremarkable mattered more.

He kept rings hidden. Veil of Ghost did that. Ring Veil Low → Veil of Ghost High → Phantom Veil Top, different name each grade. It showed two yellow hundred-year rings to world. True purple thousand-year 1350y and 1850y only Ledger saw. A boy showing two thousand-year rings would not be allowed to mend fences for bread. He learned that at second village when a boy stared too long at his hands.

Basic Walking Low had become Silent Wind Step Mid at a hundred, different name. Silent Wind Step Mid would become Ghost Frost Storm Step High at a hundred, different name again. That was why his feet did not throw dust even carrying weight. Basic Running Low had become Wind Stride Mid, different name, then would become Storm Step High, different name, then Ghost Wind Burst Top, different name. He did not run. Running was for fold in scree coming too fast. On hem road, Mountain Stride was honest work. Mountain Stride Low → Ridge Stride Mid → Wind Ridge Step High → Storm Ridge Phantom Top, different name each grade. It was not walking faster. It was walking so ground did not know you passed. Street taught it differently than ridge. On ridge, you learned where rock would give. In town, you learned where crowd would give.

He found work at market edge, where a carpenter had a stall with broken axle. The man looked at his frame and frowned. Ye Cang was eleven, but shoulders had freight height Grey Wolf bloodline gave at High, sixty-five percent, old thin line evolved five years plus thousand-year rings. Body Control Low had become Flowing Body Mid, different name, then would become Storm Body High, different name, then Ghost Body Top, different name. That was why wrist no longer sprained lifting, back held winter wood without complaint. He could lift about five hundred kilograms now, three times normal eleven-year-old, denser bone, quicker muscle, reflexes a step ahead. He did not say that. He said he could lift.

Carpenter let him try. He lifted axle, not with show, but with point leading, as Basic Spearmanship taught. Basic Spearmanship Low → Spear Flow Mid → Storm Spear High → Ghost Spear Top, different name each grade. Thrust recover low guard boar point leads moving thing hand's width complete style. Hands knew work because Basic Spear Low → Spear Flow Mid → Storm Spear High, different name each grade. Axle settled. Carpenter nodded once.

While carpenter worked, Ye Cang watched market. Observation Low → Hunter's Eye Mid → Spirit Perception High → Void Eye Top, different name each grade. He had been tallykeeper whole adult life in another world, one thing better than anyone knew. Here that became Clear Mind Mid, different name, then would become Profound Tally High, different name. He counted without meaning to: sacks of grain, coins changing hands, days to city if honest. Father said hem roads around forest skirts season to Shrek City. Same autumn boy with Spirit Eyes would walk into forest far east. Two roads meeting. Second half did not count aloud. Ledger counted.

A woman argued over price of pelts. He smelled them before seeing, whole day, because Keen Senses Mid. He knew pelts were winter fox, taken late, not prime. Hunter's Sense Low → Beast Sense Mid → Predator Sense High → Phantom Sense Top, different name each grade. Five years at Flowing Soul Cultivation Mid, then Dark Pool Cultivation High now, pool dark dense water of deeper well fed by three bloodlines and seventeen poured levels. Reborn mind wasted no hour and no motion. Method ran while he carried water, while he sat black pools, while he slept. Where other souls worked one hour, his worked every one, and it compounded. That was why Spirit Sea Realm 850 points at eleven, perception ten to hundred meters, house fly detecting hand before motion. He did not show it. On hem road, soul power flowed where eye went, body arrived where mind went first. That was Storm Frost Ghost Hunt's own effect.

Storm Frost Ghost Hunt had been Basic Combat Style Low → Grey Ridge Hunt Mid → Storm Frost Ghost Hunt High → Ghost Storm Dominion Top, different name each grade. Carpenter finished axle and paid two copper coins and half loaf. Ye Cang took it with nod Clear Speech Mid made plain. He ate half loaf while walking market edge, not middle where eyes were. One sign said Shrek City, east, ten days by cart, fifteen by foot if honest. Another said forest road closed after dark, spirit beasts. He understood. Silent Wind Step Mid made step quiet, seen-late. Wind Stride Mid would have made him fast, but fast was not honest here. Patience as Limb Mid was honest.
