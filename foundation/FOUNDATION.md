# FOUNDATION — the base of base

v1.0, 2026-09-25. Written before any chapter, at the author's word. Every
name and number in this serial is PROPOSED and strikeable until it is
written; every ruling lives verbatim in `RULINGS_LOG.md`.

## THE LESSONS (from the first serial — this serial's first laws)

1. **THE STATUS PANEL LAW.** The FULL status panel — every list, every
   line, current — exists from chapter one, night one, and
   `foundation/STATUS.md` is built before any chapter and kept current
   every turn. The first serial never printed it once. Never again.
2. **EVERYTHING COUNTED.** The innate skill, every ring's skill, every
   technique, every life-skill — each with its own meter, its own pace,
   fed by everything that grows, upgrading the instant a meter touches
   100%. No frozen meters. No missing lists.
3. **THE MIND.** The OC is a grown Earth reader who has read this era's
   whole story. He is written smart, patient, and unafraid — he plans
   ahead of the headmasters, earns what should be earned, sells what
   should be sold, and fears nothing he has already read. The smartest
   person in the room until canon itself says otherwise.
4. **FOUNDATIONS FIRST.** Nothing is written before the base locks, and
   the base locks only at the author's rulings.
5. **RECEIPTS.** Every canon claim verified before it enters a chapter
   (`CANON_GROUND.md`). Nothing on memory alone.

## THE WORLD RULES

- **Era:** the Soul Land II era — Shrek City, the Tang Sect in its
  decline, the Sun Moon Empire rising on soul tools, the Star Luo and
  Heaven Dou empires, the Great Star Dou Forest.
- **THE CANON-ADJACENCY LAW:** canon goes as canon goes where our story
  does not touch it. What our story touches, changes — and every change is
  computed, receipted, and logged. Big canon events are never bent without
  the author's word.
- **THE KNOWLEDGE LAW (this serial's Ignorance Law, re-derived):** the OC
  knows canon — up to the end of what he read. The people of the world
  know only what the world has told them. The OC may act on what he knows;
  he may never explain to a native what the native cannot know. His
  knowledge shows in his choices, never in his mouth.
- **TIMELINE PURITY:** no canon future leaks into the world's speech. The
  anime-novel reorder rule stands: the spine is the receipts, and we never
  reorder canon.
- **THE STYLE (carried whole):** plain speech; every number survives the
  author's arithmetic; panels cold and quiet at key beats, digits never
  loose in narration; nothing fixed; the working band 2,400–3,400 words
  (the author may order carries); the style gate self-enforces.
