No shop, no quests, no points, no rewards, no interest in him at all.** Not watching; just there, like the floor. It cannot be influenced by resources — nothing bought moves a meter. It is free. It never invents roads: the world defines the next stage, and the System counts what he does with it.

He names it himself — **The Ledger** (PROPOSED): a man who counted for a living, naming the thing that counts him.
