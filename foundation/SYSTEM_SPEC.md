# SYSTEM SPEC — The Ledger (working name)

v1.0, 2026-09-25. Complete from day one: every law the first serial
earned, installed before the first chapter. The System is silent — this
document is the design, not the voice.

## 1. What it is

It wakes the night of the martial-soul awakening, behind his eyes, and it
counts. **No voice. No intelligence. No shop, no quests, no points, no
rewards, no interest in him at all.** Not watching; just there, like the
floor. It cannot be influenced by resources — nothing bought moves a
meter. It is free. It never invents roads: the world defines the next
stage, and the System counts what he does with it.

He names it himself — **The Ledger** (PROPOSED): a man who counted for a
living, naming the thing that counts him.

## 2. THE STATUS PANEL (the first law of this serial)

The FULL panel — every list, every line, current — opens whenever he looks
for it, and prints at every key beat: the first night; every rank-up;
every year's end; every gate he crosses. `foundation/STATUS.md` is its
ledger and is updated the same turn any number moves; the drift guard
(`tools/check_panels.py`) fails the build on any stale line.

## 3. The lists

- **Techniques** (slotted): cultivation methods and learned arts. One slot
  per rank; one thing per slot; slotted techniques run **FULL PASSIVE,
  24/7, at his best**; learned-once, free-switch; seen once, learnable.
- **Soul Rings** (slotted): one seat per rank; a ring seats the night of
  its absorption and **AGES in its seat** (the Aging Law — the signature:
  in canon a ring's years are fixed at the kill; his grow).
- **Soul Bones** (slotted): as canon; seats by the same count.
- **Bloodline** (slotted, the fourth list): unlocks the moment a bloodline
  exists; seats and deepens like a ring. For a wolf soul, the question of
  what a bloodline could be is design-doc only until the author rules.
- **Skills** (never slotted): the spirit's own ability is the FIRST skill
  (innate, seated the night the System wakes); every ring seats exactly
  one more; each carries its own silent incantation, spoken in the mind.
- **Life-skills** (never slotted): the everything-list — senses, speech,
  walking, counting, reading, writing, and every other thing a life does.
  New lines surface when the counting does.
- **The level** (context line): the crystal's measure, counted with the
  rest.

**The Slots line:** seats per slotted list, same number in every list —
`1/1/1` at the start, `+1` at every rank-up, to `10/10/10`.

## 4. The laws (numbered, all carried)

1. **THE INITIALIZATION LAW** — a meter opens where life put it, never
   zero, never inflated. The night it wakes, it has already counted his
   six years.
2. **THE UPGRADE LAW** — the moment a meter touches 100%, the technique
   upgrades to its next stage, immediately, in place. 100% is a gate,
   never a resting state. The named method IS the next stage.
3. **THE VARIED-PACE LAW** — every skill and technique is different; each
   takes its own time, at its own pace, for its own reasons. No two meters
   alike.
4. **THE INFUSION LAW** — everything that grows feeds every open meter:
   age, growth, mastery elsewhere, cleaner spirit power, a ring's
   vitality, a bloodline. A meter never freezes while its feeders grow.
5. **THE ACQUISITION LAW** — seen once, learnable. The world's roads only.
6. **THE PASSIVE LAW** — slotted techniques run 24/7 at his best, awake
   and asleep.
7. **THE AGING LAW** — rings age in their seats (his strength, the ring's
   own potential, the bloodline, what the world weighs), and everything
   seated on a ring deepens with it — the skill keeps its identity, its
   power grows.
8. **THE RESONANCE LAW** — every seat leans on every other; the whole is
   faster than the parts can account for.
9. **THE TENDING LAW** — growth by living. Nothing is purchasable. No
   ceiling.
10. **THE MASTERY LAW** — the last part is not more work; it cares how
    true.

## 5. Panel grammar (every form)

`「Martial Soul — Name」` · `「Level — N」` · `「Slots: N / N / N」` ·
`「Technique — Name: NN%」` · `「Soul Ring — Beast: N years」` ·
`「Skill — Name: NN%」` · `「Bloodline — Line: NN%」` · life-skill lines
`「Name: NN%」` · ring-skill lines as skills. Digits live in panels and
ledgers only, never loose in narration; panels print at key beats, cold
and quiet.

## 6. What it never does

No voice, no explanations, no gifts, no tasks, no shop, no points, no
favorites. It never answers a question. It never warns. It counts — and
the boy who reads it does the rest.
